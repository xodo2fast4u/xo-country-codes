Orange marker, shared code

These codes route to more than one country or territory. The North American Numbering Plan under +1 and Russia plus Kazakhstan under +7 are the two largest examples, resolved by area code or by national routing rules rather than the country code alone.

Dimmed entries, non geographic

Codes like +800, +808 and +882 are not assigned to a country. They serve international freephone numbers, shared cost numbers and satellite or network services that operate across borders.
